About Me

A Mind Between Logic & Art

I believe great work lives at the intersection of structure and imagination — where systems think and art feels.

I am an Information Systems student with a passion for technology, business, and artistic expression.

My learning journey includes programming fundamentals, CS50, C++, and Object-Oriented Programming with C++.

Alongside my interest in technology, I have experience and learning interests in digital marketing and accounting, as well as coursework in CMA Part 1.

I am also passionate about Arabic and Latin calligraphy, typography, and advertising design.

I enjoy combining analytical thinking with artistic creativity, exploring new ideas, and expressing them through visual art and thoughtful design.
